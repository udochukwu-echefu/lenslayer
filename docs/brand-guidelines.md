# Lenslayer Brand Guidelines v1.0

## Quick reference

- Brand idea: The Lens Loop
- Primary mark: Coral inspection loop with a wine evidence layer
- Primary color: Ink Navy, `#2B2E4A`
- Signal color: Signal Coral, `#E84545`
- Primary typeface: Figtree
- Voice: Calm, rigorous, direct
- Descriptor: Evidence-led document intelligence
- Tagline: Find the clause. Follow the evidence. Decide with context.

## 1. Identity idea

The Lens Loop is an open inspection symbol with a layered terminal. The coral loop represents careful review rather than a closed conclusion: Lenslayer leaves evidence visible and conclusions reviewable. The wine layer represents the recorded finding or human decision.

The geometry is built on an eight-unit grid. It should feel precise without looking institutional, and distinctive without relying on a shield, padlock, check mark, courthouse column, or legal scale.

### Clear space

Keep clear space equal to one quarter of the mark width on all sides. For horizontal lockups, measure the clear space from the outer edge of the symbol and wordmark, not from the descriptor.

### Minimum size

- Mark: 24px digital, 8mm print
- Horizontal lockup without descriptor: 120px digital, 35mm print
- Horizontal lockup with descriptor: 160px digital, 45mm print
- App icon: 32px interface use, 180px Apple touch asset

At sizes below 160px, remove the descriptor and use the mark with the Lenslayer wordmark only.

### Do not

- Stretch, compress, rotate, crop, or reconstruct the mark
- Replace approved colors with arbitrary colors
- Add gradients, shadows, outlines, bevels, or transparency
- Place the duotone mark on photography or a low-contrast patterned surface
- Separate the terminal from the loop or change its proportions
- typeset the wordmark in another font without approval

## 3. Color system

### Core palette

| Name | Hex | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink Navy | `#2B2E4A` | 43, 46, 74 | Primary text, dark surfaces, product foundation |
| Signal Coral | `#E84545` | 232, 69, 69 | Logo loop, calls to attention, primary brand signal |
| Evidence Wine | `#903749` | 144, 55, 73 | Logo terminal, evidence states, editorial depth |
| Clause Plum | `#53354A` | 83, 53, 74 | Supporting illustration, secondary brand surface |

### Supporting neutrals

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | `#F4F1E8` | Warm brand background and reversed logo color |
| Surface | `#FCFAF6` | Raised light surfaces and report pages |

Paper and Surface are supporting neutrals derived for legibility. They do not replace the supplied four-color identity palette.

### Recommended balance

- 60% Paper or Ink Navy foundation
- 25% supporting surface and typography
- 10% Signal Coral
- 5% Evidence Wine or Clause Plum

### Accessibility

- Ink Navy on Paper: 11.68:1, WCAG AAA
- Ink Navy on Surface: 12.65:1, WCAG AAA
- Paper on Evidence Wine: 6.57:1, WCAG AA
- Paper on Clause Plum: 9.41:1, WCAG AAA
- Signal Coral on Paper: 3.46:1
- Signal Coral on Ink Navy: 3.37:1

Signal Coral is not approved for normal-size body text on Paper or Ink Navy. Use it for the logo, large display text, non-text controls with supporting labels, rules, and attention indicators. Use Ink Navy, Evidence Wine, Clause Plum, or Paper for accessible text.

## 4. Typography

Use Figtree for headings, body copy, navigation, reports, and product UI. The logo wordmark uses Figtree ExtraBold with custom tracking.

| Role | Weight | Size guidance | Line height |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Brand display | 800 | 64–112px | 0.9–1.0 |
| Marketing H1 | 800 | 48–80px | 0.95–1.05 |
| Section heading | 700–800 | 32–52px | 1.0–1.15 |
| Product page title | 700 | 32–40px | 1.1 |
| Product section | 650–700 | 20–28px | 1.2 |
| Body | 400–500 | 16–18px | 1.5–1.65 |
| Label | 700–800 | 11–13px | 1.3 |

Display tracking may tighten to `-0.05em`. Uppercase labels use `0.08em` to `0.12em`. Body copy stays at normal tracking and a maximum of 75 characters per line.

## 5. Voice and messaging

### Personality

| Trait | We are | We are not |
|---|---|---|
| Calm | Measured under consequence | Alarmist or dramatic |
| Rigorous | Evidence-linked and specific | Vague or falsely certain |
| Direct | Plain about risk and next action | Dense, legalistic, or evasive |

### Message hierarchy

1. Evidence before assertion
2. Action after explanation
3. Honest uncertainty
4. Human decision ownership

### Approved messages

- Category: Evidence-led document intelligence
- Tagline: Find the clause. Follow the evidence. Decide with context.
- Product sentence: Lenslayer turns consequential documents into traceable review decisions.
- Contract Review: Understand the clause before it becomes the problem.
- Verify: Reconcile the evidence before onboarding becomes exposure.

### Copy rules

- Say “deserves attention,” not “is illegal” or “is fraudulent,” unless verified by an authoritative process
- Say “suggested action,” not “automated decision”
- Keep risk, confidence, and verification status separate
- Name the source behind every consequential finding
- Do not claim legal advice, KYC certification, biometric verification, or regulatory approval

## 6. Visual language

### Layout

Use geometric construction, open counters, ruled evidence rows, asymmetric editorial space, and document-like alignment. Avoid repetitive card grids and decorative dashboards.

### Iconography

- 24px base grid
- 1.75px to 2px strokes
- Rounded joins with restrained corner radii
- Use outline icons for navigation and filled shapes only for strong state indicators
- Never use the logo mark as a generic bullet or status icon

### Imagery

Prefer close, legible document details, human review moments, and controlled desk environments. Avoid gavels, courthouse columns, handshake stock photography, robot imagery, glowing AI brains, and generic cybersecurity shields.

## 7. Product application

- Ink Navy is the product foundation
- Signal Coral marks active selections and attention, not every control
- Evidence Wine identifies source-linked detail and decision depth
- Clause Plum supports secondary surfaces
- Risk states must use text and icons in addition to color
- The app icon uses a coral loop and Paper terminal on Ink Navy

The files under `branding/` are the approved v1.0 sources. SVG is the master format. PNG files are exports and should never be edited directly. Brand tokens live in `branding/tokens/lenslayer-brand.css` and `branding/tokens/lenslayer-brand.json`.
