What notice do we need to give to stop the renewal?

Sources · 2

Citations checked
1Clause 3.2 · p. 4Renewal“…not less than ninety (90) days’ written notice…”
2Clause 22.1 · p. 17Notices“…by hand or recorded delivery to the registered office…”

Answer

Give written notice at least 90 days before the term ends, so by 2 Dec 2026 [1]. It must go to the supplier’s registered office by hand or recorded delivery; email alone doesn’t count [2].
